# Demo artwork

Original AI-generated fictional imagery created for Colibri Kit on 2026-11-02, distributed with this repository under its license. These are sample identities and products, not photographs of customers or endorsements. No remote image service is required.

- `portraits.jpg`: six fictional people, three columns by two rows.
- `products.jpg`: canvas tote, ceramic mug, desk organizer and linen notebook, two columns by two rows.
- `team-covers.jpg`: design, engineering and growth workspaces, three columns.

`visuals.js` and `visuals.css` provide semantic helpers and crop the sprites. Unknown user-entered names receive initials, never an unrelated stock face. Icons are original inline SVG paths in `visuals.js`. Decorative images use empty alt text; meaningful product and cover images have accessible labels.
