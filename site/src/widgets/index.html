What you are pasting

It keeps to itself

The widget draws inside its own sandbox (a shadow root). Your site's CSS cannot break it, and its CSS cannot leak into your page.

No tracking, no cookies

The script draws the widget and nothing else. It makes no network requests and stores nothing. The newsletter form posts to the address you give it, not to us.

Small and readable

Everything you typed is in the snippet itself, so you can change the text later by editing it. Prefer no script at all? Use “HTML only” on a page that loads Colibri CSS.

Free, with a small credit

Every widget carries a “Built with Colibri” link. That is the whole price. The code is MIT-licensed and on GitHub.
